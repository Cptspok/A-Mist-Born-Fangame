FINDINGS (static trace, not an automated or manual combat test)
project.godot maps secondary_action to RMB by default; configurable keybinds apply.
ContextualInput._unhandled_input routes the press/release to PlayerCombat in
equipment context, or to Allomancy while Focus is enabled. Focus/menu/slot changes
cancel guard; re-press after cancellation. Equipment creates the runtime from
the equipped ItemDefinition.combat_definition; active_slot selects its recipient.
Sword and Club both use melee_weapon.tscn and can_block=true.
Held secondary raises after attack commitment/recovery. Sword raise is 0.15s;
Club is 0.22s. Only BLOCKING protects, not RAISING or GUARD_BROKEN.
EnemyMeleeAttack samples the player Hurtbox and calls its DamageSource. Knife
Fighter source damage is 16, pressure 10; Heavy is 42, pressure 45. Their source
nodes are on the attacker, not on the sampled hit volume. Projectile moves its
source to contact before applying damage. Hurtbox calls the parent's CombatEquipment
defend_damage with source position/pressure before HealthComponent.apply_damage.
The active melee runtime compares horizontal camera forward with horizontal
source direction. Sword protects +/-55 degrees, Club +/-50 degrees, including
the boundary. Out-of-cone/zero-horizontal-direction hits receive full damage.

The existing path is connected and the defense math was functional by inspection;
no missing dispatch or damage bypass was found for Knife Fighter/Heavy. Gameplay
confirmation remains with the owner. Fixed an edge case: invalid/nonpositive or
already-dead-target damage could react against/break guard before Health rejected
the damage. Hurtbox now rejects those inputs before defense and presentation.

Knife Fighter            16                   2.4               4

Ordinary damage fraction is Sword 0.15 / Club 0.25.
Pressure above strength uses max(chip fraction, 1 - strength / pressure).
Strength is Sword 20 / Club 16. Heavy therefore overwhelms both; existing impulse
is min(8, (pressure-strength)*0.15), followed by 0.65s exposed recovery and a new
raise if still held. No accumulating meter. Side/rear hits do not overwhelm guard.
Health subtracts the defended amount, clamps at zero, emits health_changed/death,
and returns actual HP loss. Pewter defers 50% of that amount while burning;
later debt payments bypass reprocessing. ARMOR currently has no registered damage
processor: 032's reference to armor processing was inaccurate. No armor change here.

FEEDBACK
New combat_feedback.gd is a small mouse-ignoring Control owned by PlayerCombat's
CanvasLayer, independent of debug displays. Outcome is presentation-only runtime
metadata; it does not feed into damage math. Incoming valid Hurtbox hits show:
- Normal: red screen border and HIT.
- Successful block: cyan shield outline and BLOCKED, retaining weapon recoil.
- Overwhelm: orange cracked shield and GUARD OVERWHELMED; existing weapon drop,
  knockback and recovery remain.
- Directional failure while guard is ready: pink edge flash, inward chevrons and
  GUARD BYPASSED; full damage remains.
Guard status also shows QUEUED, RAISING, READY or RECOVERING, so holding RMB is not
mistaken for immediate protection during commitment/raise. No numeric debug data.
Feedback uses shape/text as well as color; incoming indications last 0.6s, or 0.8s
for overwhelm. New hits replace the previous incoming indicator.
