# Blended capture, damped dynamic hold and controlled Push geometry

2026-09-17, following 022. Owner manual feedback: visible hold stutter,
horizontal-then-vertical approach and unexpectedly low/downward launches.
The owner explicitly authorized replacing the internal follow implementation.
No successful manual acceptance result is claimed for this implementation.

Diagnosis from code (not a reproduced runtime trace)
- 022 moved a kinematic-frozen RigidBody with move_and_collide every physics
  tick, directly to its target after a short entry. Physics-tick transforms
  were drawn without interpolation; project-wide interpolation is not enabled.
  This presents discrete steps against a render-rate mouse-look camera and
  provides no persistent damped follow-through. RigidBody synchronization and
  contact clipping can add discontinuities. Those mechanisms have been removed;
  which contributed most to the owner's observed stutter remains unmeasured.
- The entry radius was 2 m, inside/behind the 2.2 m held position. Arresting
  approach and lifting there could produce the disconnected right-angle path.
- Project gravity is 24 m/s^2. Authored Steel force is 48 and LIGHT object
  coupling is 2, yielding at most 96 N on the 1 kg prototype. Even a correctly
  horizontal continuous force from rest gives an immediate downward component
  (~14 degrees without terminal softening). Geometry alone cannot supply a
  forward initial velocity. The old implementation erased velocity at release.

Replacement
- scripts/loose_metal_capture.gd retains one established eligible capture but
  replaces frozen positional following with an implicit critically damped motor.
- New scripts/loose_metal_body.gd owns _integrate_forces on the two existing
  prototype loose-body scenes. It delegates only when captured. No root/body
  transforms are assigned, no reparenting or freeze/custom_integrator changes.
  Gravity, collisions, CCD, mass and ordinary physics remain active throughout.
- Default entry is now 3.5 m horizontal range, +/-1.6 m vertical reach, after
  normal acquisition and established active Iron. Entry itself preserves motion.
  Smooth control weight increases monotonically with time (0.25 s) and distance
  (3.5 down to 2.5 m). Normal Iron force is weighted by 1-control_weight;
  elevated control and gravity compensation grow with control_weight. Thus
  lifting begins outside the 2.2 m held position while approach is still active.
  This weighting is exclusive to capture transition; free Iron callers use 1.
- An implicit timestep-dependent spring/damper update runs only in the body's
  physics integration callback, not a competing transform update. Responsiveness
  is 16/s and controller acceleration is bounded at 250 m/s^2. Partial player
  translational feed-forward (80%, bounded at 12 m/s) keeps running/jump lag
  small. Camera angular velocity is not fed forward. Angular motion is damped.
- Body physics poses are interpolated into runtime draw-only mesh proxies.
  Original mesh/tether/collider hierarchies remain physically authoritative;
  the original meshes' render layers are hidden only at runtime. This avoids
  moving the loose bar's tether, which is parented beneath its mesh.
- Presentation compensates only the player's tick-stepped translation while
  held, fading out over 0.1 s on release. This prevents interpolated world poses
  and an uninterpolated FPS camera producing relative translation judder.
  Camera rotation is never compensated: visible turn follow-through remains.
- Debug wireframes, labels and axis endpoints use the same presentation poses.
  Debug text displays capture control percentage and physical COM measurements.
  There are no global interpolation, physics-rate or player motor changes.

Velocity ownership / release
- The motor tracks its own velocity contribution against successive physics
  observations. Genuine gravity/contact/external-force changes are accounted
  separately and dissipated by the same authored hold damping. Compensated
  gravity is not stored as latent falling momentum. Incoming momentum is not
  resurrected from a stale snapshot when releasing.
- Drop removes the tracked controller contribution, disconnects the callback
  and restores can_sleep. It does not globally zero velocity, freeze a body or
  apply a release force. Remaining free motion and gravity resume naturally.
- Invalid/deleted/disabled objects, burn/depletion, context changes, death/locks,
  reset and scene teardown retain cancellation. Body tree exit also releases.
- Separation is bookkeeping for this authored controller, not an exact inverse
  of arbitrary nonlinear contact solving. Test releases during collisions and
  strong external impulses; normal uncluttered jump/turn follow should not be
  retained as projectile velocity. Detailed behavior remains manually untested.

Control geometry and Push
- FREE torso origin stays Player/AllomanticOrigin at local (0,0.35,0).
- Added Player/CameraPivot/Camera3D/CapturedPushOrigin, local (0,-0.3,0).
  Capture interprets that offset in its clamped aim frame (-35..55 degrees).
- Neutral COM target = that control origin + (0,0,-2.2). At the authored
  standing eye height 1.6 m above feet, control origin/COM are at about 1.3 m.
  Their connecting line is parallel to neutral sight, 0.3 m below it.
- CapturePoint Position Z tunes held distance; X/Y tune additional offsets,
  default zero. Distance is at least four bounding radii, clamped 1.5..3.5 m.
  The actual COM, not an arbitrary mesh origin, remains the physical endpoint.
- On captured Steel: snapshot control origin and actual physical COM; end Iron
  and remove controller contribution; evaluate the existing Steel law using
  saved positions and cleaned velocities; apply through PhysicalForceResponse.
- Capture-only release adds an impulse equal to the evaluated existing Steel
  force times push_impulse_duration (0.12 s). This is a deliberate new local
  release behavior, needed to provide forward initial momentum under 24 gravity;
  it is not a retune of FREE Steel. Both participant couplings and body masses
  remain authoritative. At full response a 1 kg LIGHT prop receives 11.52 Ns.
  Terminal response can reduce it. Set duration to 0 for force-only release.
- No camera-forward velocity assignment or upward angle/gravity compensation
  is applied after release. The impulse direction comes from the saved physical
  origin-to-COM axis. Later held-Steel ticks use the ordinary free torso axis.
  No second force application or cleanup follows the launch within that tick.

Collision / limitations
- A test-only sweep of actual body collision shapes clips the desired goal.
  Native dynamics/CCD resolve actual motion; no manual transform pushes through
  geometry. Sustained obstructed full control (>0.5 s) or >5 m separation drops.
- Sharp turns can encounter the player's body; there is no pathfinding/orbit
  solver. High-speed arrivals, moving obstacles and tight spaces need testing.
- Visual interpolation carries about one physics tick of presentation delay;
  diagnostic physics coordinates and rendered positions may differ slightly.
  Draw proxies support the current static loose meshes, not skinned/animated
  content. New eligible prop resources need the reusable loose_metal_body script.
- Early Push during transition or during large aim lag uses the actual unsettled
  geometry. The parallel-axis goal applies to stable hold, not arbitrary lag.
