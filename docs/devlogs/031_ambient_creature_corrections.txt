Ambient Creature correction pass
Implemented 2026-09-21. Follows 030; owner reports the system works but reaction
range was too small and one-shot groups left the city empty.

Changes
- AmbientCreatureDefinition.trigger_radius default: 3.0 -> 4.5 m.
- resources/ambient_rat.tres explicitly sets 4.5 m; ambient_bird.tres: 4 -> 7 m.
  Tune each Resource's trigger_radius in Inspector; profile does not override it.
- AmbientCreatureSpawner now exposes respawn_enabled=true (replaces respawn),
  respawn_delay=20 s, respawn_min_player_distance=12 m. Other fields retained.
  No existing serialized uses of the old respawn property were found.
- Existing live count decrements on each creature's one-shot tree_exited signal.
  Only zero count schedules cooldown. One owned, one-shot Timer handles cooldown
  and later 1-second clearance retries. Waiting/live-count guards block duplicates.
  Deferred scheduling guards scene teardown. Invalid/missing visuals do not spawn.
- After cooldown, all Node3D members of player group must be outside 12 m plus
  the scaled spawn radius from the spawner origin (conservative area clearance).
  Missing players cause continued waiting, including during player replacement.
  No distance check runs while a group is populated or its cooldown is running.
- respawn_enabled=false preserves one-shot behavior; disabling during a pending
  wait cancels respawning at the next timeout. Initial spawn_on_ready is unchanged.
- Mini City's MarketClutterRats, WestAlleyRats, LoadingLaneRats, WestRoofBirds and
  EastRoofBirds explicitly use enabled=true, delay=20, player clearance=12.
  Counts, positions and spawn radii unchanged.

Preserved
Creature movement/flee/fade/collision script unchanged. Actor and thrown-object
recognition unchanged, including minimum_object_speed=2.5 and no stationary-object
reaction. No navigation, visibility tests, per-frame searches or perception added.
Hathsin untouched. No changes to unrelated gameplay or other Mini City content.

Validation
Static lifecycle/data review and Godot 4.7.2 --check-only on the spawner passed
without parse errors. Godot reported sandbox-denied user log-file access.
Git diff whitespace check passed. No startup/gameplay automation or test harness.
Manual testing of this correction remains pending.

Suggested manual checks
Approach rats (4.5 m) and birds (7 m). Empty a group; remain nearby beyond 20 s:
it must stay empty. Move beyond the 12 m clearance plus spawn radius: within
about 1 s it should return. Repeat to check no duplicate groups. Disable respawn:
the group should stay gone. Verify fast thrown metal still triggers and stationary
metal does not. These are suggested checks, not recorded test results.
