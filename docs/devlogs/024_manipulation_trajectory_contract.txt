# Explicit loose-metal approach and aimed captured release

2026-09-17. Replaces the strength-blended arrival from 023 following owner
diagnosis and implementation instructions. Manual gameplay acceptance pending.

APPROACH contract
- FREE -> APPROACH -> HOLD -> RELEASED. Established eligible Iron acquisition
  is still required; first acquisition tick uses unchanged free Iron.
- Assistance starts at the existing 3.5 m minimum range, extended when the
  incoming closing speed needs more lift/braking runway. The extra runway is
  closing_speed * lift_time / 2 + closing_speed^2 / (2 * acceleration), measured
  outside the horizontal hold distance. Vertical eligibility remains 1.6 m.
- At the first solver callback, sample actual COM and incoming velocity. One
  cubic Hermite segment starts there with that velocity and ends at the initial
  hold point with zero relative velocity. Duration uses horizontal displacement,
  incoming speed/braking and required lift, bounded by 0.18..0.8 seconds.
- A damped endpoint offset follows the evolving hold destination and player
  frame velocity. Its Hermite weight and derivative are zero at entry; at arrival
  it contributes full endpoint position and frame velocity. No per-frame restart.
- Static endpoint, ground-level start with zero vertical velocity:
    y(s) = y0 + (3s^2 - 2s^3) * (hold_y - y0)
    dy/dt = 6s(1-s) * (hold_y - y0) / duration
  Thus an elevated target requests upward motion throughout 0 < s < 1, while
  the same parameter schedules horizontal approach/braking. Lift is not delayed
  until a separate positional handoff.
- The dynamic body receives reference displacement per physics step plus
  non-oscillating positional-error recovery. Recovery is bounded per component;
  horizontal error cannot consume vertical authority. Standard Jolt damping and
  gravity are compensated separately, outside tracking acceleration budgets.
- No ordinary Iron force is submitted during APPROACH/HOLD. There is no
  (1-blend) Iron + blend capture-force trajectory anymore.
- HOLD requires completed reference time, no current blockage, <0.12 m actual
  position error and <0.6 m/s velocity error relative to the moving hold frame.
  Reference velocity must also have converged. Crossing entry range is not HOLD.

Collisions and hold
- Test-only shape sweep covers the upcoming physical displacement, not the
  entire route to the final hold point. Blocking travel limits that step only.
  Separating floor contacts do not erase upward motion. The goal is never
  replaced by collision-limited travel. Persistent obstruction releases after
  0.5 seconds. Native rigid-body collisions and existing CCD remain active.
- The existing HOLD spring/damper, responsiveness, acceleration limit and 80%
  bounded player translation feed-forward are retained. No freeze, custom
  integrator switch, root transform writes or camera parenting was introduced.
- loose_metal_body.gd and its presentation interpolation were not edited.

Captured Steel contract
- Snapshot n = -clamped_capture_aim_basis.z, physical COM P and impulse duration.
- Use virtual_origin = P - n; evaluation direction P - virtual_origin is n,
  regardless of height/position error relative to the ideal hold destination.
- End Iron/capture and clean velocity before evaluating terminal response.
  Existing Steel strength, terminal law, LIGHT coupling, mass and player recoil
  remain in the shared evaluation/physical response APIs. Existing 0.12-second
  force-equivalent impulse remains; no arbitrary speed or upward angle added.
- The launch consumes the current primary-input engagement. No further Steel
  force is applied from that press, even if the same target stays in the cone.
  Primary release rearms ordinary Steel; context cancellation resets the latch.
  Launched metal immediately returns to gravity and ordinary world physics.
- Iron release without Steel remains a drop. Releasing Iron before issuing a
  Steel press ends capture; this change does not create a post-drop aim grace.

Velocity ownership
- Track expected post-step velocity including standard damping and gravity.
  Deviations are observed world/contact/external-force motion; incoming and
  observed momentum decay under the existing capture damping.
- Remove output minus retained free velocity on release. This removes planned
  approach motion, camera/player following and pending gravity compensation,
  without globally zeroing genuine momentum or restoring an entry snapshot.
- Actual external momentum can still affect total release velocity. The Steel
  impulse axis itself is always the captured aim axis. Contact decomposition
  remains an estimate around a nonlinear solver, not exact impulse provenance.
