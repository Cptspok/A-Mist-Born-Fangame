# Loose Metal Capture v0.1

Implemented 2026-09-17 after 020. The developer reported that free Iron drew
ground-level props toward the feet, making continued targeting and useful Push
alignment awkward. This pass adds one deliberate captured control state after
an established free Pull. It does not replace the chest-origin force law.

Files changed
- scripts/loose_metal_capture.gd (new): force motor, marker tuning, eligibility,
  constrained aim position, obstruction/break checks and state release.
- scripts/allomancy_controller.gd: single maintained Pull/capture/released Push
  lifecycle, existing ability cancellation integration and Iron usage charging.
- scripts/allomancy_targeting.gd: separately validated maintained target path.
- scripts/metal_tether_component.gd: capture_enabled opt-in, false by default;
  deleted collision-volume guard in availability checks.
- scripts/allomancy_debug.gd: captured-state text and hold-motor indication.
- scenes/player.tscn: CapturePoint Marker3D/script under AllomanticOrigin.
- scenes/allomancy_light.tscn and scenes/world_loose_steel.tscn: enable capture
  on their existing loose-object tethers; no transforms or physics values changed.
- docs/devlogs/README.md and this chronological record.

Eligibility
- Explicit capture_enabled, available LIGHT tether, actual live RigidBody3D,
  unfrozen, ordinary physics integration, mass <= 2 and bounding radius <= 0.65 m.
- Radius includes enabled direct CollisionShape3D children and their offsets.
  Missing collision shapes are rejected. CharacterBody/HealthComponent ancestors
  are rejected. Opt-in is only authored on the existing LIGHT box and loose bar;
  arbitrary tethers, actors and equipped objects are not enabled automatically.
- MEDIUM/HEAVY stay free. Their existing couplings are (0.35,1) and (0.85,0.25),
  compared with LIGHT (0.03,2); the existing Medium also has enlarged geometry.
  Retaining their systemic player/object response is the conservative v0.1 choice.
  ANCHORED is always rejected, regardless of the opt-in flag.

Acquire -> free Pull -> capture
- Initial acquisition keeps nearby candidates, camera cone/LOS and range.
- While active Iron applies to an eligible acquired prop, remember that prop.
  It can enter capture on a subsequent physics tick, once its physical body
  origin is within capture_radius (2.2 m) of AllomanticOrigin, with Steel inactive.
- Maintained targeting drops the camera-cone requirement, but requires a valid
  available tether/body, the existing max_range and unobstructed chest-to-tether
  LOS against the existing world layer. It cannot maintain through a wall.
- Free pulling before capture still uses AllomanticInteraction unchanged. No
  camera pitch/aim vector is used in free Steel or Iron force evaluation.

Capture tuning / physics
- Select Player/AllomanticOrigin/CapturePoint in the player scene.
  Transform Position defaults to (0,0.15,-1.4), i.e. 1.4 m forward and 0.15 m
  above the chest origin in neutral aim. Parent origin retains crouch following.
- Marker script Inspector defaults: capture_radius 2.2 m; follow_responsiveness
  12 /s; maximum_acceleration 400 m/s^2; break_distance 3.5 m; pitch -35..55 deg;
  maximum_mass 2; maximum_object_radius 0.65 m.
- The relatively high but finite acceleration budget brakes incoming props
  near the existing free 24 m/s terminal before they cross the player's body.
  These are initial authored values, not measured/playtested balance results.
- Only after capture, camera pitch (clamped above) rotates the marker offset
  with player yaw. Offset bounds are forward 1.2..2 m, lateral +/-0.3 m and
  vertical -0.2..0.4 m, preserving a small region rather than free-flight control.
- An implicit critically damped spring applies a mass-scaled, bounded continuous
  force through PhysicalForceResponse. Translation feed-forward uses player
  velocity; camera angular velocity is not injected. Actual gravity from the
  physics direct state is compensated by force, not by disabling gravity.
- A conservative bounding-sphere sweep shortens an obstructed goal. Prolonged
  obstruction (>0.5 s) or excessive distance breaks capture. Native collisions
  remain active, including collisions with the player. No transform/velocity
  assignments, reparenting, collision exceptions, gravity overrides, freezing,
  or extra launch impulses are used. Only one prop is held.
- Holding charges existing Iron consumption even at steady rest. It replaces
  the free Iron force for that prop while captured, not the global Iron law.

Release / cleanup
- Release Iron: existing ability deactivation removes the motor and references;
  existing momentum and ordinary gravity/collision remain. No new drop key.
- Steel while captured: remember this same prop, end Iron/capture first, then
  apply the unchanged Steel force from its actual physical axis. Keep the
  released prop targeted under range/LOS while Steel stays active. There is
  no fake projectile velocity or bonus launch impulse. Releasing/repressing
  Iron is required for another Pull; a held RMB cannot immediately recapture.
- Invalid/deleted/disabled objects, lost LOS, burn/access/depletion, context
  cancellation, gameplay locks/death/reset and scene removal clear control.
  A broken captured hold ends Iron activation; a fresh press can reacquire.
- Since body physics properties are never changed, there is no suspended state
  or collision exception that could survive teardown and leave a floating prop.

Validation and manual limitations
- Static architecture/diff inspection and git diff --check passed.
- Exactly one minimal headless application/menu startup exited 0, with no
  script/resource errors and a system certificate-store warning. The shell
  preloads the world/player resources; this is not a gameplay/physics test.
- No gameplay harness, separate project, automated camera/traversal, repeated
  physics runs or desktop/editor interaction was performed. Detailed feel and
  visuals remain untested. Final braking defaults were selected by code review.
- Watch high-speed arrivals and player contact, rapid aim reversals, crouching
  near floors, narrow doorways, rotated bars, wall-adjacent captures and fast
  player launches. The bounding sphere is conservative for long/thin shapes;
  native collisions still resolve starting overlaps. Obstructions may drop
  an object sooner than its exact shape would require. Extreme external motion
  may outrun the motor and break capture. Object rotation is not stabilized.
- Pressing Steel early during the settling transition uses the real, still
  settling position and therefore may not yet align with the final aim point.
- No damage, stagger, breakage, throw ability or combat effects were added.
  Free force laws, reserves/rates, Pewter, movement/crouch, inventory/equipment,
  dialogue, checkpoints and level layouts were not edited. No Levels/Hathsin
  file, geometry, material or hierarchy was modified, restored or cleaned up.
